## [6.0.0] — 2026-06-26
### VIT Network v6.0.0 — VIT Chain Launch
- **VIT Chain Core**: Initial release of the VIT Chain (L2) with support for PoS consensus and on-chain settlements.
- **P2P Network**: Decentralized peer-to-peer networking layer for block propagation and state sync.
- **Block Explorer**: New analytics suite for real-time monitoring of VIT Chain blocks, transactions, and nodes.
- **Cloud Status Dashboard**: 8-subsystem health monitoring for identity, AI, core, database, task, blockchain, tachyon, and infrastructure.
- **VITCoin Direct Sale**: Integrated direct purchase engine using the 3-Governor hybrid pricing model.
- **Tachyon VESS Integration**: Enhanced decentralized storage verification with swarm maintenance workers.
