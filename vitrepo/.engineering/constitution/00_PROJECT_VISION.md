# 00 Project Vision: VIT Network

## 1. The Vision
VIT is an "Institutional Operating System" and Intelligence Platform. It is evolving from a sports prediction application into a multi-vertical, cloud-native ecosystem spanning Research, Education, Business, AI, and Blockchain.

## 2. Core Purpose
To provide high-fidelity, verifiable intelligence through a decentralized network of AI agents and human validators.

## 3. High-Level Goals
- **Precision**: Deliver 90%+ accurate signals for sports and niche prediction markets.
- **Verifiability**: Anchor all critical intelligence results on-chain.
- **Scalability**: Support continental-scale data ingestion and analysis.
- **Professionalism**: Adhere to institutional design principles and high-density data visualizations.

## 4. Key Verticals
- **Sports Intelligence**: AI-driven match analysis and affiliate execution.
- **Niche Prediction Markets**: Governance, Elections, and Merit-based forecasting.
- **Research & Academy**: Advanced reasoning agents for academic and policy simulation.
- **Blockchain Infrastructure**: L2 settlement, Proof of Storage (Tachyon), and Decentralized Identity (DID).
