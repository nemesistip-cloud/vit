# $VIT Tokenomics: The Analytics Fuel

## Utility
1. **Access**: Pay $VIT for high-confidence premium signals.
2. **Loyalty**: Automated loyalty attestations recorded on-chain.
3. **Staking**: Secure the Oracle network and earn rewards.
4. **Governance**: Vote on policy forecasts and platform direction.
5. **Commissions**: Agents earn $VIT for facilitating transactions.

## Mechanics
- **Max Supply**: 1,000,000,000 VIT.
- **Deflationary**: Percentage of all marketplace fees are burned.
- **Staking Yield**: Derived from platform revenue and treasury growth.

## Distribution
- **Ecosystem & Growth**: 40%
- **Staking Rewards**: 20%
- **Team & Advisors**: 15% (vested over 3 years)
- **Initial Liquidity**: 15%
- **Community Airdrop**: 10%
