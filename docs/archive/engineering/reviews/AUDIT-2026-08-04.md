## 2. vit-chain Detailed Probe

**Block sample (height 1843–1845):**
- `tx_count: 0` across all sampled blocks — no real transactions yet
- `storage_proofs: []` — Proof-of-Storage challenges not generating on-chain proofs
- `consensus_votes: []` — single validator, no voting quorum
- `block_reward: 10 VIT` per block to genesis validator
- Genesis validator: `0x94bec23d7dc4ce0b9bb5cb7f30bf695daffa6165`
- Stake: `185302 VIT` (genesis allocation)

**Assessment:** Chain is live and producing blocks on schedule (15s epochs). Core consensus engine is functional. However, the "Proof-of-Storage" mechanism is incomplete — storage proofs are not being generated or submitted. This is the most significant gap between the technical claims and the live system.
